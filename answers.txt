1) Pros and Cons of Agile development:
    - Pros:
        - Adaptibility: Improvements are done through small ranges in delivery dates, so projects change easily and adapt quickly
        - Transparency: Potential issues and improvements become obvious through the production. Everyone communicates clearly and issues are caught/fixed quickly as well as improvements become clearly
    - Cons:
        - Lack of documentation: Documentation becomes less of a priority than the constant planning/progress. Responsibilities like billing and record keeping become slowly done.
        - Goal focus shifting: Focus shifts constantly depending on which parts of the project require more attention. This means everyone is not focused on a single goal but rather what needs to be done first.

2) Role/Responsibility of Scrum Master vs Product Owner
    - Scrum Master:
        - Facilitate and Coach who ensures the Scrum is followed and helps improve workflows
        - Guiding ceremonies, fostering collaboration, and supporting communication
        - Create scrum events, seek ways to enchance performance and agility

    - Product Owner:
        - Develop and communicate product goal
        - Create and communicate product backlog items
        - Ordering product backlog items
        - Ensure product backlog is transparent, visible, understood

3) 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle:
    - Sprint planning
        - Establish what your Scrum team wants to accomplish in the sprint
        - Plan the sprint, assign tasks, set deadlines
    - Daily standup meeting
        - Each member should answer: what did I accomplish yesterday? What will I accomplish today?
        - Should be a quick everyday meeting where everyone speaks
    - Sprint review
        - Product owner and other stakeholders respond and evaluate work
        - Add work from review to backlog and potentially include in next sprint
